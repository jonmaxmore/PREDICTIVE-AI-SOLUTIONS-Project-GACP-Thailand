# WHT (Withholding Tax 3%) — STUB Implementation

**Batch:** Iter 26 (hardening loop, 2026-05-16)
**Status:** STUB — data collection + certificate recording only.
**Owner:** ACCOUNT_PLATFORM team (Predictive AI Solution Co., Ltd.)

This document explains what the WHT subsystem does today, what it
deliberately does **not** do, the legal anchors, and the roadmap to a
full implementation.

---

## 1. Legal references

| Anchor | Subject |
|--------|---------|
| ป.รัษฎากร ม.50 (Thai Revenue Code §50) | Duty of the PAYER to withhold tax on payments for services. Standard rate for service fees paid by a juristic person to a juristic person is **3%** of the service fee (pre-VAT). |
| ป.รัษฎากร ม.69 ทวิ | Duty of the PAYER to issue **ทบ.50 ทวิ** (หนังสือรับรองการหักภาษี ณ ที่จ่าย / Withholding-Tax Certificate) to the payee at the moment of deduction, in 4 copies (RD, payee × 2, payer). |
| ป.รัษฎากร ม.59 + ม.83/8 | Monthly **ภ.ง.ด.53** remittance — payer aggregates all WHTs deducted during the month and files with RD by the 7th (paper) / 15th (e-Filing) of the following month. |
| ป.รัษฎากร ม.77/1 (10) | State revenue (เงินรายได้แผ่นดิน, the DTAM state fee) is VAT-exempt and **not subject to WHT** — WHT obligations attach only to taxable commercial transactions. |
| ป.รัษฎากร ม.91/1 | Criminal liability for failing to remit collected tax to RD. |

## 2. Who withholds what — context for GACP

The GACP platform issues **two** documents per phase (see
`apps/backend/config/invoice-issuers.js`):

1. **ใบเสร็จเงินรายได้แผ่นดิน** (DTAM state fee) — VAT-exempt, **WHT-exempt**.
   The applicant pays กรมบัญชีกลาง directly; no withholding involved.
2. **ใบกำกับภาษีเต็มรูป** (PLATFORM service fee + 7% VAT) — issued by
   Predictive AI Solution Co., Ltd. This **is** the document that may
   bear WHT, but only when the applicant is a **juristic person**
   (company / cooperative). Individual farmer applicants have no WHT
   obligation under ม.50.

| Applicant type | WHT applies? | Why |
|----------------|--------------|-----|
| INDIVIDUAL (natural-person farmer) | **No** | ม.50 attaches to juristic payers only. |
| JURISTIC (company / co-op as buyer) | **Yes** | Buyer self-withholds 3%, remits to RD on ภ.ง.ด.53. |
| COMMUNITY_ENTERPRISE | **No** (default) | DOAE-registered, not VAT-juristic. Documented as IF; treat as INDIVIDUAL. |

The platform is **the seller / payee** in this relationship. The
withholding duty belongs to the buyer; we never act as withholder.

## 4. What's NOT in scope (intentional deferral)

### 4.1 Auto-withholding at point of sale — DEFERRED

The PLATFORM invoice always charges the **full gross** (subtotal + 7%
VAT). We do **not** deduct 3% inside the invoice nor change the cash
flow at payment time. Reasoning:

- WHT is a duty of the PAYER, not the SELLER. Auto-deducting would
  invert that legal model.
- If the platform held the 3% but did **not** remit it monthly via a
  working ภ.ง.ด.53 pipeline, that would be unremitted collected tax —
  criminal exposure under ม.91/1 and a serious legal failure.
- Owner direction (Tier 9 note, `invoice-issuers.js`):
  > "ถ้าหัก 3% แล้วเสี่ยงผิดกฎหมาย หรือเราไม่ได้นำส่ง เอาออกก็ได้"

### 4.2 Monthly ภ.ง.ด.53 filing pipeline — DEFERRED

We don't aggregate WHTs into a monthly RD-filing pack. We are the
payee on ภ.ง.ด.53 (the **buyer** files it), so the platform has no
duty to emit this file from our side. When the platform itself acts
as PAYER (e.g. paying our own vendors for office supplies), a future
purchase-invoice + ภ.ง.ด.3/53 module will handle that — separate work.

### 4.3 ทบ.50 ทวิ PDF generation — OUT OF SCOPE

The certificate is the buyer's document; they produce it on their own
finance system and mail it to us. We only **receive** and **record**.

### 4.4 Journal posting at cert-receipt time — OPTIONAL, NOT WIRED

`recordWhtCertificate({ recordJournal: true })` is plumbed for a
future hook on `journal-entry-service.recordWhtEntry(...)` that would
post:

```
Dr. WHT Receivable (1320-001 — Tax Credit Receivable)   <whtAmount>
  Cr. Revenue — Platform Fee (4110-001)                       <whtAmount>
```

…reclassifying recognised revenue as a prepaid tax credit the
platform will offset against its own ภ.ง.ด.50 (corporate income tax)
year-end return. Today the hook emits a `[wht][journal-stub]` log
line so finance can post the entry manually at month-end. The chart
of accounts in `journal-entry-service.js` does **not** yet define
1320-001; that's the first thing the future iter must add.

## 7. Future-implementation roadmap

When the business is ready to act as a withholding payer (e.g. when
buying services from external juristic vendors) **OR** when DTAM/RD
asks for full ภ.ง.ด.53 evidence from the platform's side:

1. **Promote storage** from `Invoice.metadata.whtCertificate` (Json)
   to a dedicated `wht_certificates` table — straight column-
   extraction; the JSON shape was designed for this.
2. **Wire `journal-entry-service.recordWhtEntry`** + add `1320-001
   WHT Receivable` to the chart of accounts.
3. **Build the ภ.ง.ด.53 generator** (sister to vat-report-service's
   ภ.พ.30): aggregate certs by month, emit RD-portal CSV.
4. **Consider auto-deduction** at invoice-creation time **only if**
   the platform takes on the payer role for some service category
   AND has a tested monthly remittance path.
