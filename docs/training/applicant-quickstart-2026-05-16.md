# Applicant Quickstart — GACP Certification

**Audience**: Herbal medicine producers applying for GACP certification
**Estimated reading**: 15 minutes
**Languages**: Thai (primary), English (this doc)

> Welcome. This guide walks you through every step from signup to receiving your GACP certificate. Total processing time is typically 60–90 days from first payment.

---

## 1. Before you start

### 1.1 What is GACP?

GACP (Good Agricultural and Collection Practices) is a national standard issued by the Department of Thai Traditional and Alternative Medicine (DTAM). It certifies your farm meets requirements for:
- Safe and traceable herbal medicine production
- Sustainable farming practices
- Documentation and quality control

A GACP certificate opens access to domestic and export markets that require certified suppliers.

### 1.2 Prerequisites

- Functional Thai citizen ID (or registered juristic person)
- Farm with established crop (at least one harvest cycle completed)
- Basic farm records (planting log, harvest log)
- Phone number capable of receiving SMS/email
- Ability to receive on-site visit by DTAM auditor

### 1.3 Cost summary

| Stage | Amount | When |
|-------|--------|------|
| Phase 1 (document review) | 25,000 THB | At application submission |
| Phase 2 (on-site audit) | 5,000 THB | After Phase 1 approval |
| Re-audit (if FAIL) | 5,000 THB | Within 30 days of FAIL |
| Renewal (every 3 years) | 25,000 THB | Before expiry |

All payments go to: **กรมการแพทย์แผนไทยฯ** account 123-4-56789-0 (Krungthai)

## 2. Step 1 — Sign up

[FIG-01: Signup page]

1. Visit https://gacp.dtam.go.th
2. Click "สมัครสมาชิก" / "Sign Up"
3. Enter:
   - Full name
   - Thai national ID
   - Phone number
   - Email
   - Password (12+ characters, must include letter + number + symbol)
4. Verify email (check spam folder if no email in 10 min)
5. Verify phone via OTP
6. Set up 2-factor authentication (optional but recommended)

---

## 3. Step 2 — Submit application

[FIG-02: Application wizard step 1]

### 3.1 Application sections

1. **Applicant info**: pre-filled from signup; verify
2. **Farm info**:
   - Location (GPS coordinates auto-captured from device or pinned on map)
   - Total area (rai/ngan/wah)
   - Crop(s) cultivated
   - Number of plots
3. **Crop details** per plot:
   - Species (select from list)
   - Variety
   - Planting date
   - Harvest method
4. **Documents to upload**:
   - Title deed / land lease (PDF)
   - Farm map (image or PDF)
   - Recent photos of farm (at least 5)
   - Existing farming records (planting log, harvest log) if available
5. **Self-assessment checklist**: 9 sections of GACP standard
6. **Declarations**: legal acknowledgements

### 3.2 Save and continue

The system saves drafts automatically. You can return anytime within 30 days. After 30 days, drafts expire.

### 3.3 Submit

When all sections complete + green:
1. Review summary page carefully
2. Tap "Submit Application"
3. Application enters status SUBMITTED
4. You receive confirmation email + SMS

---

## 4. Step 3 — Payment (2 transfers per phase)

### 4.1 Phase 1 payment (25,000 THB)

[FIG-03: Payment instructions screen]

1. After submission, you'll see payment instructions
2. Transfer 25,000 THB to:
   - Account: **123-4-56789-0**
   - Bank: **Krungthai (กรุงไทย)**
   - Name: **กรมการแพทย์แผนไทยฯ**
3. Take screenshot or photo of bank slip
4. Return to application > Payment > Upload Slip
5. Upload slip image (JPG/PNG, max 5MB)
6. Enter slip reference number from your bank statement
7. Tap "Confirm Upload"

The slip enters DTAM Finance review queue. Decision within 4 working hours (typically same day).

If APPROVED: application moves to DOC_REVIEW status.
If REJECTED: you'll see reason; correct and re-upload.

### 4.2 Phase 1 outcome

DTAM reviews your documents within 7 working days:
- **APPROVED**: application moves to PHASE_2_PAYMENT_DUE
- **CHANGES REQUESTED**: you have 14 days to update and re-submit
- **REJECTED**: application closed; you may re-apply after 90 days

### 4.3 Phase 2 payment (5,000 THB)

Same process as Phase 1, but 5,000 THB.

After payment approved, you move to QUEUED status (awaiting auditor assignment).

### 4.4 Why two transfers?

GACP fees are non-refundable once a phase begins. Splitting payment means:
- You only pay Phase 2 when Phase 1 review confirms you can proceed
- DTAM accounts the funds correctly per phase
- You always know what stage you're at

## 5. Step 4 — On-site audit

[FIG-04: Audit scheduling email]

### 5.1 Scheduling

Within 2 weeks of Phase 2 payment approval, DTAM Scheduler will:
1. Assign an auditor
2. Send you an email + SMS with:
   - Auditor's name and contact
   - Proposed audit window (3-day range)
3. You pick your preferred day within the window
4. Confirm or request reschedule (max 2 reschedules)

### 5.2 Preparing for the audit

Before audit day:
- [ ] Have all farm records ready (planting, harvest, treatments)
- [ ] Have title deed or land lease document
- [ ] Have receipts for inputs (seeds, fertilizer, pesticide)
- [ ] Ensure access path for auditor's vehicle
- [ ] Have at least one farm worker available to answer questions
- [ ] Allow 4–6 hours for the audit

### 5.3 During the audit

- Greet auditor and verify their DTAM ID badge
- Show all areas: cultivation, storage, processing, sanitation facilities
- Be honest — auditors detect inconsistencies
- Photos: auditor takes photos with watermarked field app
- Sign auditor's checklist at the end

### 5.4 After the audit

The auditor submits the report on the field app. You receive the result within 24 hours:
- **PASS**: certificate workflow starts
- **FAIL**: detailed report with non-conformities; you may pay 5,000 THB for re-audit within 30 days
- **NEEDS_REVIEW**: senior auditor reviews; outcome within 5 working days

---

## 6. Step 5 — Receive your certificate

[FIG-05: Certificate download page]

### 6.1 Issuance

After PASS:
- System generates a digitally signed PDF certificate (RSA-2048)
- You receive download link by email + SMS
- Certificate also available in your applicant portal forever
- Validity: 3 years from issuance date

### 6.2 Verify your certificate

Anyone can verify your certificate's authenticity:
1. Visit https://gacp.dtam.go.th/verify
2. Enter certificate ID (printed on cert)
3. Or scan QR code on cert with phone camera

If verification fails, the cert is forged or revoked.

### 6.3 Physical copy

You can request a printed copy (free first copy, 200 THB additional copies):
- Applicant portal > My Certificate > Request Printed
- Delivery within 14 days by registered post

---

## 7. Step 6 — Renewal

[FIG-06: Renewal reminder]

### 7.1 Timing

You'll receive reminders:
- 90 days before expiry
- 60 days before expiry
- 30 days before expiry
- 7 days before expiry

Start renewal process at least 60 days before expiry to avoid gap.

### 7.2 Process

1. Login to portal
2. Tap "Start Renewal" (only visible within 90 days of expiry)
3. Review and update farm info (changes since last cert)
4. Pay 25,000 THB (renewal phase 1)
5. Document review (typically faster, 3 working days)
6. Pay 5,000 THB (renewal phase 2)
7. On-site audit (typically lighter scope)
8. Receive new certificate (validity 3 more years)

### 7.3 Expired certificate

If your certificate expires without renewal:
- Status changes to EXPIRED
- You must apply fresh (not renewal)
- Fresh application fees apply
- Cannot claim "renewal lineage" for marketing

## 9. Common questions

**Q: I lost my password.**
A: There is no account recovery (operator decision 2026-09-17). You cannot reset the password yourself, DTAM staff have no procedure to set a new one for you, and nothing is sent by email or SMS (operator decision 2026-09-16). Keep the password you set at registration safe.

**Q: My slip was rejected. What now?**
A: Read rejection reason carefully. Common: wrong amount, wrong account, blurry image. Re-upload corrected slip. No additional fee.

**Q: Can I change my farm address after applying?**
A: Before Phase 1 approval: yes, edit application. After approval: contact support; may require re-audit.

**Q: My audit failed. Can I appeal?**
A: Yes, contact support within 14 days with evidence. Appeal reviewed by senior auditor panel.

**Q: My certificate is missing some details. Why?**
A: Certificate format is standardized by DTAM. Custom fields not allowed. If real error: contact support.

**Q: How do I prove certificate to buyer?**
A: Share verification URL or QR code. Buyer can verify instantly online.
